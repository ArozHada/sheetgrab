SheetGrab

Clip job postings, internships and papers from any page into your own Google Sheet, in one click.

Coming soon to the Chrome Web Store.

How it works

  1. Connect your Google account and paste the link to your tracking Sheet.
  2. Map your columns (Company, Role, Deadline, Contact, Link…). SheetGrab guesses the mapping from your headers and can create missing columns for you.
  3. Clip. On any job posting, click the SheetGrab icon. The fields are pre-filled from the page. Fix anything you like, then click Save to Sheet.

Works anywhere

SheetGrab doesn't depend on any one website's layout. It looks for labels like “Deadline:”, “Apply by” or “Hiring Manager”, and uses standard page metadata, so it works on LinkedIn, job boards, company career pages and research paper sites.

Private by design

  • The page is read only when you click the icon.
  • Data goes straight from your browser to your own Google Sheet. There are no SheetGrab servers.
  • No analytics, no ads, no tracking.
