Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 7, 2026

SheetGrab is a Chrome extension that copies details from the web page you're viewing (for example a job title, company, deadline and link) into a Google Sheet that you choose. This policy explains what data SheetGrab touches and where it goes. In short: your data goes only from your browser to your own Google Sheet. SheetGrab has no servers and collects nothing.

What SheetGrab reads

  • The current page, only when you click the SheetGrab icon. SheetGrab reads that tab's text, title, URL and standard page metadata so it can pre-fill the form. It never reads pages in the background or tracks your browsing.
  • Your chosen Google Sheet. SheetGrab reads the tab names and the header row (row 1) of the spreadsheet whose URL you enter, so it can map columns.

What SheetGrab writes

  • When you click Save to Sheet, SheetGrab appends one row, containing the values shown in the form (which you can edit first), to the spreadsheet tab you selected.
  • If your tab is missing a column you asked for in Settings, SheetGrab adds that header to row 1. It never deletes or overwrites your existing data.

Where your data goes

  • Page details are sent only to the Google Sheets API (sheets.googleapis.com), directly from your browser, and only to write to your own spreadsheet.
  • SheetGrab has no servers of its own. The developer never receives your page content, Sheet data or account details.
  • SheetGrab uses no analytics, advertising, tracking or third-party services.
  • SheetGrab does not sell, share or transfer your data to anyone.

Google account access

SheetGrab asks for the Google permission https://www.googleapis.com/auth/spreadsheets ("See, edit, create and delete your Google Sheets spreadsheets"). Google requires this permission to read headers and append rows. SheetGrab only uses it on the single spreadsheet you configure, and only to do the things described above. Sign-in is handled by Chrome's built-in Google sign-in. The access token is managed by Chrome and is never sent anywhere except to Google's APIs.

SheetGrab's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs to any other app will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

What SheetGrab stores

Your settings (spreadsheet ID and name, tab name, and your column-to-rule mapping) are saved with Chrome's chrome.storage.sync, so they follow you across browsers where you're signed in to Chrome. Page content is not stored by the extension. Once a row is saved, it lives only in your Google Sheet.

Your choices

  • Click Disconnect in SheetGrab's settings to sign out and revoke the access token.
  • You can also remove SheetGrab's access at any time from your Google Account permissions page.
  • Uninstalling the extension removes its settings from Chrome.

Children

SheetGrab is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect any personal information.
